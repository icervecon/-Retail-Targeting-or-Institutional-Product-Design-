Retail Targeting or Institutional Product Design? Distribution Structures in European UCITS Funds
Public replication notebook.

This notebook reproduces the main descriptive and regression evidence using the public de-identified replication dataset ucits_public_replication_dataset_v2.xlsx. The original LSEG data are proprietary and are not included. Management-group classifications were manually harmonized by the author to consolidate multiple legal entities and country-specific management companies belonging to the same asset-management organization. The harmonized groups are intended to capture economically meaningful management platforms rather than individual legal entities reported by LSEG.
Management groups are symbolized as follow: Amundi MG001;  Waystone	MG002; Eurizon MG003; Apex Fundrock MG004; Raiffeisen MG005;  Carne MG006; Allianz MG007; Aberdeen MG008; DWS MG009; UBS MG010; BNP MG011; Union Investment MG012; Mercer MG013; Blackrock MG014; Natixis MG015. 

This preserves fixed effects, clustered standard errors, and within-group aggregation while removing original management-group names.
